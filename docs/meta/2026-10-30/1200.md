# Meta pass, 2026-10-30 12:00 (first pass; covers the title-scene work since about 06:00)

## Hour in brief

The title screen went through four cities (modern pastel, Japanese, castle town v1, castle town v2): it was deployed
as the studio title and the Rampage scene was started. Then Kevin asked to productionise the design flow (dogfood the
game-designer skills, add an hourly meta layer), installed Ultimate Monsters, and asked for Drive sync and a repo scan.

## Patterns

1. **Packs first, not hand-built.** Asked for a Rampage monster, I hand-built an ape, a pixel sprite and a lizard.
   Kevin: "I don't think we need to create our own here ... you should have been nudged toward using our existing
   resource packs." Earlier the same shape happened with Japanese buildings (procedural, because no pack fitted).
2. **Show options, then build.** Repeated across the day: three framings per city, a pack thumbnail page before the
   castle town, lineups of creatures before choosing. Kevin picks from visuals; he redirects fast when shown choices.
3. **Repetition reads as low quality.** Called out three times ("still way too much repetition", "buildings look even
   more repetitive", "small lengths of wall are too repetitive"). Fixes: cloned variants, roof tints, walls stretched
   tower to tower, irregular streets and wall.
4. **Taste: natural and period-right, no tech artefacts.** Rejected: gradients on buildings, pure white building
   shells, glass pushed too hard, cranes in castle times, perfect symmetry, a repetitive river. Kept: pastel gruvbox,
   flat tones, real streets, things that grow in believable steps (scaffolding layer by layer).
5. **One scene at a time, with sign-off.** Kevin keeps the order explicit ("keep focusing on the city", "start on the
   rampage scene instead of EB") and wants 3 variations per step.
6. **Many mid-turn messages.** Kevin queues requests while I work (10 in one hour). Handled by acknowledging each in
   one line and doing them in order; nothing was dropped.
7. **Hiccups:** a montage sorted t10 before t4 (misread the timeline); `pkill -f` killed its own shell (exit 144);
   a Drive pack hit "Quota exceeded" (fixed via the same bundle on Poly Pizza); duplicate model names overwrote files;
   capture and record scripts lived in a scratch folder all day.
