## Patterns
- **Pop-in is the recurring complaint.** "don't have them just appear out of nowhere", "people appear on the wall and
  then disappear", "don't just have it snap", the poster "includes the kaiju walking into frame". It is the same taste
  as last hour's "enter from the ocean": every appearance, disappearance and change of direction needs a cause and a
  transition.
- **The blinking soldiers were a culling bug, not choreography.** Cloned skinned meshes kept stale bounds. A probe
  showed identical state at 3 s and 5 s, and `frustumCulled = false` fixed it.
- **Generous hit tests hid mistakes.** The tap test scanned the screen and found taps well above the kaiju's head
  counting. The scan was worth keeping as a tool.
- **Debug probes were done by hand.** Temporary `window.__x = ...` sed injections (men, D, posAt) were added and
  reverted four times. Product code is out of scope for this pass, so I noted it instead of changing it.
- **Hiccup:** `claude plugin install` from the shell wrote to ~/.claude (no CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR). The project-scope
  settings made it load for ~/.claude-2 too (checked).
