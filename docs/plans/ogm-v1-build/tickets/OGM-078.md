## Outcome

On a game screen the owner presses Invite and the device's share sheet (or a copied link) carries a `/join/<token>` link; a family member who opens it lands, after sign-in, in the room as a watcher who sees the owner's edits arrive live with the version badge and "talking to the builder…" while the owner is in a private panel, can follow the owner's camera or look freely, is dropped into play when the owner presses Play, and sees "<owner> left" with the paused-then-ended state if the owner drops; the owner sees who is in, can kick, and can end the room.

## Context and evidence

Rooms are relay-only, the owner's browser is the authority, the Colyseus client lives in the host app, never the iframe (E07-2, E07-4, D-ROOMS). Invite links replace guest codes; every joiner is a signed-in adult passing the age screen and the family allowlist (E07-7, A2); OGM-052 owns the token exchange and the `/join` route. Watchers receive the design transaction stream after the backend accepted it and catch up from a snapshot; OGM-053 owns the client, forwarding and `resync` (E07-5, R73). Private panels never reach the wire: `presence.privateMode` zeroes cursor, selection and camera; `presence.building` carries only a label and a percentage (E07-6, R76). Owner drop → `pausedForHost`, 120 s, then `ended: host-left` (E07-8, R77, A5). Play is "on receipt of one message" (E07-10). Resolved: no host migration; `watcher` is phase-derived, not a role; room size is per game, default 4, ceiling 8 (E07-1).

## Scope

- `InviteButton` (64 px, icon-only with `aria-label`) registered through OGM-075's `gameExtras` (`placement: 'topbar'`): creates the room if none (`POST /api/games/:g/rooms { branchId, buildId, maxPlayers }`), then `POST /api/rooms/:id/invites` → `url`; `navigator.share({ url })` when available, else copy to clipboard with a 2 s confirmation; a members popover (name, connected, playerIndex) with Kick (owner) and End room.
- `RoomEntry`: mounted by OGM-052's `/join` route after it has the grant and pin; shows "Joining <game>…", calls OGM-053's client to join, handles `JoinRejection` codes (`BUILD_MISMATCH` → reload, `ROOM_FULL` → the max, `GRANT_*` → ask for a new link) and then renders the game screen (OGM-075) with the room overlay.
- `WatcherOverlay` during `phase: 'editing'` for non-owners: version badge (branch name + derived version from the pin), a camera toggle Follow / Free (Follow applies the owner's `presence.camera`; Free leaves the local editor camera), the owner's selection highlight, "talking to the builder…" when `presence.privateMode`, "building… <label> <pct>%" from `presence.building`, and `resync` shown as a brief "catching up".
- Phase handling for every member: `playing` switches the frame to play within one message; `migrating` shows "loading the new build" until `draft.ready`; `pausedForHost` shows "<owner>'s connection dropped" with a countdown; `ended` shows the reason (`host-left` → "<owner> left", `owner-closed`, `idle`, `revoked`) and the member's own last autosave time when the SDK reported one, with Leave back to `/games`.
- Owner side: the room badge with member count; `phase` to `playing` on Play (through OGM-053's `send('phase')`); End room (`owner-closed`).
- `RoomSession` interface over OGM-053's client so components take an observable, and a `fakeSession()` behind `?test` for e2e; `window.__ogmHost.room = { phase, members, presence }`.

## Failure, security, migration and accessibility cases

A rejected join shows the reason and a way out; a WebSocket drop during `editing` shows "reconnecting" from OGM-053's state and never a blank frame; `resync` is a short notice. The grant and invite URL are handled only in memory; the share sheet gets the URL and nothing else; names shown come from `Membership.name` (no email, E07-7). Kick and End are owner-only in the UI and enforced by the server. No migrations. 64 px Invite, Play, Leave; 44 px Kick, camera toggle; status changes announced through `aria-live`; the countdown is text, not colour; `prefers-reduced-motion` removes the catching-up animation.
