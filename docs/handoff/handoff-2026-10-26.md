### Prototype (`prototypes/ui-v0/`, throwaway code)

- **Built:**
  - a paper-cut home screen (wind, music, a Start transition);
  - a dungeon editor (paint, place, move, inspector, undo/redo, torches, a chest);
  - a play loop (hero with a sword, goblin AI, hits, win and lose);
  - phone layout;
  - an e2e test (`tools/e2e.mjs`).
- **Changed after Kevin's review:**
  - **One pack:** the Synty goblins were replaced by Kenney Mini Dungeon's `character-orc`, which shares the hero's rig and animations. Models went from 13.1 MB to under 1 MB, and the Synty license issue is gone. No Synty files are served anywhere; the old deploys were deleted.
  - **Sound on iPhone:** MP3 instead of Ogg, `navigator.audioSession.type = 'playback'`, audio unlocked on `touchend`/`pointerup`, and the first tap on the sound chip now always turns sound on.
  - **Darker dungeon music** (Kenney "Retro Mystic").
  - Removed the torch that blocked the spawn corridor; saved maps are migrated.
  - Grass tufts redrawn as fans of curved blades.
  - Beating the goblins no longer ends the run. The win screen comes once every chest is open.
  - **Twin-stick controls:**
    - desktop: mouse to aim, click to swing;
    - phones: the right stick aims, a tap swings, and the nub stays put.
  - **Icons:**
    - the GM sticker is the favicon;
    - the GM splatter tile is the phone home-screen and manifest icon;
    - both sources are in `prototypes/ui-v0/art/`.
  - **iOS:**
    - backgrounds are full-bleed and controls stay inside the safe area;
    - the home-screen app fills the whole screen;
    - Safari's bars are tinted by fixed edge strips (cream on top; green at the bottom on the home screen).
- **Deploys:** `netlify deploy --prod --dir prototypes/ui-v0/site --site "$(cat prototypes/ui-v0/.netlify-site-id)"`. The Netlify login and the gitignored `site/assets/` (models, audio, images) **exist only on Kevin's second machine**. From Maker-PC you can edit code, but you can't deploy until the assets are copied. `tools/copy-assets.sh` rebuilds them from `assets-local/`, which also exists only there.
